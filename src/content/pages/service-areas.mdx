## How our deadbolt locksmith service areas work

We built Deadbolt Fixers around one type of lock rather than one city. Our deadbolt locksmith service areas cover the United States through many local teams, and we match each request to the team that can reach the address with the right parts for that job.

That changes how booking works compared with a single-town locksmith. Instead of assuming coverage, we confirm it.

1. **You send the address and the problem.** City, state, ZIP and what the lock is doing.
2. **We confirm who can reach you and when.** If we cannot serve the address well, we say so up front.
3. **You get a written quote.** It reflects your area's labor rates and travel, so there are no surprise charges at the door.
4. **Our technician verifies access and does the work.** Identity checks apply to any job that opens or rekeys a lock.

## What changes from state to state

The deadbolt itself works the same in Maine and in Arizona. The rules around it do not. Three things vary by location, and they can affect your job.

### Locksmith licensing

Some states require locksmiths to be licensed, and others leave it unregulated. A few cities and counties add their own requirements.

Before anyone works on your locks, it is reasonable to ask whether your state requires a license and, if it does, to check the number with the state board. The Federal Trade Commission has warned that some locksmiths advertising locally are not local at all, and our [lockout service page](/deadbolt-lockout-service/) explains how to spot that kind of operator.

### Building codes for exit doors

The International Residential Code and NFPA 101 Life Safety Code are model codes. Each state or city decides which edition to adopt and how to amend it. That matters most for [double cylinder deadbolts](/double-cylinder-deadbolt-installation/), since a lock that needs a key to open from inside can conflict with egress rules. The local building department is the authority for your address.

### Climate and door materials

Weather changes how doors behave. Humidity swells wood doors, frost heave shifts frames, and strong sun can warp fiberglass and vinyl. These conditions are a common reason a deadbolt suddenly stops lining up, which our [guide to a deadbolt that won't lock](/deadbolt-guides/deadbolt-wont-lock/) explains.
