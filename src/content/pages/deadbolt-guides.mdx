## Key deadbolt terms, defined

These terms appear throughout our guides. Each one is explained in more depth where it matters most.

- **Deadbolt**: a lock whose bolt is moved only by a key, thumb turn or motor, not by a spring, so it cannot be pushed back by force on the bolt itself.
- **Throw**: how far the bolt extends out of the door edge when locked. Residential deadbolts are commonly made with a 1 inch throw.
- **Backset**: the distance from the edge of the door to the center of the bore hole. The two common sizes are 2-3/8 inches and 2-3/4 inches.
- **Strike plate**: the metal plate on the door frame that receives the bolt. Its screws decide how much force the frame can resist.
- **Single cylinder**: keyed outside, thumb turn inside. **Double cylinder**: keyed on both sides.
- **Grade**: an ANSI/BHMA performance rating. Residential deadbolts tested to A156.40 get a letter (A, B or C) for security, durability and finish, so "AAA" is top marks in all three. Many deadbolts also carry a Grade 1, 2 or 3 under A156.36, the auxiliary lock standard.
- **Rekeying**: changing the pins inside a cylinder so a new key works and old keys do not, without replacing the lock.
- **Keyway**: the shape of the key slot. Locks share keys only if their keyways match.

## How we write our deadbolt guides

Big home-improvement sites cover deadbolts in a paragraph or two. The deadbolt guides from Deadbolt Fixers go further because deadbolts are the only thing we work on.

- **Sourced facts.** Grades come from the ANSI/BHMA standards, code guidance comes from the model codes published by the ICC and NFPA, and dimensions come from manufacturer installation instructions. Each guide lists its sources.
- **Field context.** We add what our technicians actually find on doors: loose strike screws, sagging hinges, cylinders installed upside down, smart locks fighting a misaligned frame.
- **Honest limits.** When a fix needs a professional, the guide says so instead of stretching a DIY tip too far.
- **No invented numbers.** If we cannot source a statistic or a price, it does not appear.
