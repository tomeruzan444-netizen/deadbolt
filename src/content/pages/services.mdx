## How to choose the right deadbolt lock service

Most people search for a symptom, not a service name. Use this table to match what you are seeing to the visit that fixes it. If two rows sound right, start with the first one, since a technician can always step up to the bigger job once the lock is in hand.

| What you are dealing with | The service that fits | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The door has no deadbolt at all | [New deadbolt installation](/deadbolt-lock-installation/) | Needs a face bore and edge bore drilled into the door |
| The deadbolt sticks, grinds or will not fully extend | [Deadbolt repair](/deadbolt-lock-repair/) | Often alignment or wear that can be fixed without new hardware |
| You are locked out right now | [Lockout service](/deadbolt-lockout-service/) | Non-destructive entry first, after proof of residence |
| You moved in, lost a key or a tenant left | [Rekeying](/deadbolt-rekeying/) | New pins and keys, same lock body |
| The lock is worn out, damaged or too weak | [Replacement](/deadbolt-replacement/) | A new deadbolt in the existing holes, often a higher grade |
| You want a keypad or app control | [Smart deadbolt installation](/smart-deadbolt-installation/) | Needs good door alignment and the right connectivity choice |
| Glass sits within reach of the lock | [Double cylinder installation](/double-cylinder-deadbolt-installation/) | Keyed on both sides, after a local code check |
| You want serious pick and drill resistance | [High security deadbolt](/high-security-deadbolt/) | UL 437 cylinders and restricted keys |
| A key snapped off inside the lock | [Broken key extraction](/broken-key-in-deadbolt/) | Pull the fragment without damaging the cylinder |
| You carry too many keys | [Keyed alike deadbolts](/keyed-alike-deadbolts/) | One key for every door, often without new locks |
| You manage a storefront or office | [Commercial deadbolt locks](/commercial-deadbolt-locks/) | Heavier grades, mortise options and staff key control |
| The door feels like it would give way if kicked | [Strike plate reinforcement](/deadbolt-strike-plate-reinforcement/) | Long screws and stronger strikes anchor the bolt to the framing |

## What every deadbolt service visit includes

Whatever the job, our deadbolt lock services follow the same ground rules, so you know what to expect before a technician arrives.

- **A diagnosis before a quote.** We look at the lock, the strike and the door fit first, because the visible symptom is not always the real cause.
- **A written price before work starts.** The quote names the hardware, the labor and any after-hours or travel charges. If something changes, we stop and re-quote.
- **Identity checks for any job that opens or rekeys a lock.** We confirm you have the right to access the property, which protects you as much as it protects us.
- **Clean, reversible work where possible.** We open locks without drilling whenever a non-destructive method works, and we reuse sound hardware rather than replacing it by default.

## Residential and commercial deadbolt lock services

Homes and businesses need different things from the same type of lock. A front door on a house usually needs a solid residential deadbolt, a reinforced strike and keys that the family can manage. A busy storefront may need heavier commercial hardware, egress-friendly operation and a way to control who has keys when staff change.

Our residential deadbolt lock services focus on the doors families use every day, including rentals and multi-unit buildings where landlords rekey between tenants. Commercial work is covered on the [commercial deadbolt locks](/commercial-deadbolt-locks/) page, including mortise deadbolts, access control and hardware that suits accessibility requirements.
