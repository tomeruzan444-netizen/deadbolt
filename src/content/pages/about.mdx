## Why we became deadbolt specialists

The deadbolt is the lock that keeps a door shut when someone pushes on it. A spring latch on a knob can be slipped with a card.

A properly installed deadbolt with a full 1-inch throw, seated in a solid strike, cannot. Yet on service calls, deadbolts are where we find the most problems: bolts that only extend halfway, strikes held on with short screws, cylinders that have not seen a key change in decades.

Generalist locksmiths split their time across cars, safes, access systems and every lock in between. Deadbolt Fixers was set up to do one thing very well instead. Being deadbolt specialists means our technicians recognize a failing Kwikset tailpiece by feel, know which Schlage cylinders accept which keyways, and can tell from the scuff marks on a strike which way a door has sagged.

## What focus means for you

Narrow focus is not a slogan. It changes what happens at your door.

- **The right parts on the first visit.** Technicians stock deadbolt cylinders, bolts, strikes, reinforcement plates and long screws, not a little of everything.
- **Diagnosis before sales.** Many "broken" deadbolts are fine. The door has shifted and the bolt is rubbing the strike. We fix the cause rather than selling a lock that will fail the same way.
- **Advice that matches the door.** Grade, backset, door thickness, glass near the lock and local egress rules all decide which deadbolt is right. Deadbolt specialists are trained to check every one.

## Our standards

Every claim on this website, and every recommendation a technician makes, follows a few rules.

- **We follow the published standards.** Grades refer to [ANSI/BHMA ratings](/deadbolt-guides/deadbolt-grades-ansi-bhma/), and high security claims refer to UL 437 listings, not marketing words.
- **We respect the codes.** We will not install a keyed-inside deadbolt on an exit door without telling you about egress rules and asking you to check local code. See our [double cylinder installation page](/double-cylinder-deadbolt-installation/) for how we handle it.
- **We never invent numbers.** Prices on this site are explained by their factors, and any dollar figures are sourced national ranges, as in our [installation cost guide](/deadbolt-guides/deadbolt-installation-cost/).
- **We protect the person who lives there.** Proof of residence before a lockout opening is not negotiable, which our [lockout service page](/deadbolt-lockout-service/) explains.
